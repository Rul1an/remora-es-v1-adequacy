## What this measures, and what it does not

It measures which of the agreed seeded faults in `checker.py` the pinned corpus tells apart from the unmodified
checker. It does not establish that the checker or its inference rules are correct, anything about production use
(the suite's synthetic-premise boundary applies), or independent validation. The fault list is a selection agreed
between us, so the counts below are only as good as that list. A survivor means the corpus cannot tell that fault
apart; it does not mean the checker has it.

## What the survivors say

1. **Four guard reasons are never an expected reason**: `scope_unaccepted_or_missing`,
   `admission_presence_unknown`, `route_observation_unaccepted_or_missing`,
   `protected_effect_observation_unaccepted`. Removing those guards, changing those reason strings, or changing
   their guidance goes undetected by the cases. The runner's own checks catch one of them
   (`admission_presence_unknown` removal, through `failures`) but not the other three.
2. **Two compound-guard halves**: dropping `effect_seen` from the first admission guard survives every row, because
   no case sets `effect_seen` to anything but `true` (A05 fails on `effect_source_accepted`). Dropping
   `"expected_state" not in o` survives rows 1 and 2; row 3 exposes it only by crashing (see above).
3. **Guard precedence is pinned at two points only.** 14 of 16 order swaps survive. Every `not_established` case
   except B03 fails exactly one listed premise, so swapping two guards changes nothing unless a case fails both.
   The two that are killed are the B03 pair (control and attribution) and the B01 precedence.
4. **Premise typing**: all three truthiness mutants survive because no case gives a premise a truthy non-boolean
   value (B07's `protected_effect_observed: null` is falsy either way). The README treats these fields as trusted
   synthetic fixture premises, so this may be out of scope by design; your call.
5. **Guidance is pinned only by the per-case row.** The runner's checks score status and reason, not guidance, so
   all 23 guidance mutants survive row 3, "guidance never attached" included. The README property "Every
   inconclusive verdict carries `missing_evidence` and `decisive_if` fields" is therefore pinned only when those
   fields are scored per case: row 2 catches 19 of 23, and the four it misses are the unreached reasons in (1).

The full runner adds two kills over the cases alone: the presence-unknown guard removal, through `failures`
(one vector moved), and the `expected_state` half, through a crash.
